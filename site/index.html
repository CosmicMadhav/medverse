Why might doctors differ in their approach?

Speciality bias plays a major role: surgeons often recommend surgical resolution to fix anatomical degeneration, whereas physio/rehab physicians first attempt conservative muscular strengthening. MedVerse never ranks doctors; it empowers you with balanced understanding.

Medical Governance

We explain. Doctors decide.

MedVerse is strictly an assistive health literacy engine. It never pretends to be a medical professional, never issues diagnostic verdicts, and never picks a winner in second opinions.

  • Critical threshold values (e.g. K⁺ > 6.0) trigger immediate hospital call banners with NO AI simplification.
  • Every simplified insight is line-anchored back to the exact physical source line in your original scan.
  • Zero doctor ranking: Differences are presented with neutral clinical context to foster doctor-patient dialogue.
  • Encrypted data residency inside India (Supabase ap-south-1) with one-click export & erase options.
