MORE THAN REPORTS

Daily care, not just paperwork.

Health is mostly what happens between doctor visits. Scroll to see what MedVerse does on the ordinary days.

01

Write down BP and sugar

Log blood pressure, sugar, weight, oxygen and pulse. Each reading is coloured, with a chart of the last 30 and a 7-day average. A very high or very low reading says what to do and shows a Call 108 button. One tap sends the list to your doctor.

02

Medicines that remind you

Photograph the strip and MedVerse fills in the name, the type and the expiry date for you to check. Every dose is reminded on your phone, even with no internet. Connect a Bluetooth pill box and opening a lid ticks the dose.

03

Never miss a vaccine

Add a child’s date of birth and MedVerse lays out India’s national vaccine schedule: what is done, what is due and what is overdue. Tick each one when it is given. You get a reminder a week before and on the day.

04

Cards you can find in a hurry

Keep a photo and the number of your health insurance, Ayushman Bharat or ID card. Add the expiry date and you are reminded 30 days, 7 days and on the day. Cards never go on a shared health card or into an export.

05

Let family look after a parent

A parent makes a one-time code. A son or daughter enters it and can see whether today’s doses were taken, what is running low, the next doctor visit and the latest BP and sugar. They can only look, never change, and never see reports, photos or cards. Either side can stop it any time.

06

When help is needed

SOS texts your emergency contact by itself: a map link to where you are, your blood group, allergies and medicines. The emergency screen lists the nearest hospitals, pharmacies and blood banks with Directions and Call buttons. Turn on the lock-screen card and a helper can read your blood group and scan a QR without unlocking the phone.

07

Works when the network does not

Open your saved records, tick doses and add medicines or readings with no internet. The changes wait on your phone and are sent as soon as you are online, even if the app was closed or the phone restarted in between.

A DESIGN WE ARE WORKING ON

A pill box that gives one pill at a time.

Six medicines, each in its own tank. The box reminds you, turns a small wheel and drops one pill into a drawer. Two light beams count it. If two come out, it stops and tells you.

  • Small enough for a bedside table: 20 × 20 × 15.6 cm.
  • Pour pills into a wide tank. No picking them one by one.
  • A touch screen, a light bar and a drawer, in English and Hindi.

Honest status: this is a design, drawn and checked on a computer. It is not built or tested with real pills yet, and it is not a medical device.

Captions are in English and Hindi: use the CC button in the player.

IN AN EMERGENCY

Hold to call for help.

SOS needs a deliberate press, so it can’t go off by accident, but it is always one hold away.

  1. Press and hold SOS for 1.5 seconds. A quick tap only shows a hint.
  2. A 5-second countdown starts with a loud siren and a big Cancel button, so a slip of the thumb doesn’t call anyone.
  3. If you don’t cancel, your phone’s dialer opens with 108 ready, and MedVerse texts your emergency contact by itself: a map link to where you are, your blood group, allergies and medicines.
  4. The emergency screen then lists the nearest hospitals with directions and a call button, so you can reach one fast.

The text needs SMS permission and uses your normal SMS plan. It only goes to the contact you saved, and only when SOS fires or you tap the button.

Try it on the phone at the top: hold SOS.

OUR PROMISE

We explain. Doctors decide.

MedVerse helps you understand your records. It does not diagnose, it does not prescribe, and it never says which doctor is right.

Urgent values go to a doctor

If a result is dangerous, like potassium above 6.0, MedVerse shows “see a doctor today” and doesn’t try to explain it away.

Every explanation shows its source

Each plain sentence links back to the line it came from in your report.

No doctor is ranked

Differences are shown side by side so you can talk them through with your doctor.

Stored in India, visible only to you

Your records live in a private account on servers in Mumbai. Only you can open them, and sensitive records can be hidden behind a PIN.

QUESTIONS FAMILIES ASK

Before you download

Is MedVerse free?

Yes, it’s free to download and use.

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

You install it as an APK file straight from this page, because it isn’t on the Play Store yet. Android will ask your permission to install it, which is normal. Only download it from here.

Who can see my family’s records?

Your records are kept in a private account on servers in Mumbai, India. Only you can open them, and sensitive records can be hidden behind a PIN. To explain a report, its photo and text are read by AI services (Google Cloud Vision and Groq). Our privacy policy has the details. Read our full Privacy Policy

Does it work without internet?

Mostly yes. Saved records, reminders, ticking doses and adding medicines or readings work without internet, and the changes are sent later. Reading a new report, the AI explanations, comparisons and nearby places need internet. SOS opens your phone’s dialer for 108, which works wherever you have a mobile network.

Will MedVerse tell me what illness I have?

No. It explains what your reports and prescriptions already say. Only your doctor can diagnose you or decide your treatment.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with Android 7.0 or newer. An iPhone version is not available yet.

How do I scan a report or prescription?

Take a clear photo of your report or prescription with the app. MedVerse reads the text (this needs internet) and explains the medical terms in plain words.

Can I manage records for my whole family?

Yes. Add separate profiles for your parents, children and spouse, and keep each person’s records, medicines and doctor visits in one app.

How does the two-doctor comparison work?

Add prescriptions from two doctors and MedVerse lists what they agree on, like the tests, and what is different, like the medicines. It also flags two similar painkillers.

Can my son or daughter see my medicines?

Only if you choose. Make a one-time code in Profile and give it to them. They can then see today’s doses, low stock, your next visit and your latest BP and sugar. They cannot change anything and never see your reports, photos, cards or private vault. You can stop it at any time.

Does MedVerse track my location?

No. It reads your location only when you open Nearby help or when SOS fires, and does not save it to your account. If you switch on SOS texts, the location goes to your emergency contact in that message, and for a few minutes after if the app stays open.

Can I connect a smart pill box?

The app can connect to a Bluetooth pill box that follows MedVerse’s open format, send it your schedule and tick doses when a lid opens. There is a demo box in the app to try it, and the format and a sample ESP32 sketch are in our project files. We have not tested it on a real box yet.
