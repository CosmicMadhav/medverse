WHY WE BUILT IT

Families get the prescriptions. Not the understanding.

We sat with 31 families across Rajasthan. Reports from one lab, prescriptions from another hospital, two doctors saying different things — and no one explaining any of it.

84%

Cannot understand their lab reports without asking someone else

68%

Carry paper files from doctor to doctor, losing parts along the way

42%

Took two medicines of the same type prescribed by different doctors

90%

Rely on one person in the family to remember everyone’s doses

Field study · 31 families in Pratapgarh, Chittorgarh and Udaipur, Rajasthan (2025–2026).

OUR PROMISE

Built to help. Never to diagnose.

We believe Indian families deserve plain explanations, not internet diagnoses that cause panic. Medverse is designed around four clear guardrails:

Explains, doesn’t diagnose

The app never guesses what illness you have or prescribes medicines. It explains what your reports already say so you can talk to your doctor with confidence.

Always shows the source

Every explanation points back to the exact line in your original report or prescription, so you or your doctor can verify it in seconds.

Private on your phone

Your family’s health records belong only to you. Encrypted, locked behind your PIN, and never shared with insurers, employers or advertisers.

Works without internet

Saved records, reminders and your emergency health card work even when there is no mobile network — because emergencies don’t wait for internet.

QUESTIONS YOU MIGHT HAVE

Frequently asked questions

Is Medverse free?

Yes. Medverse is free to download and use.

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

Medverse comes directly from us as an APK file. Because it isn’t on the Play Store yet, Android asks your permission before installing — that is normal. Only download it from this website.

Who can see my family’s records?

Your records are encrypted, stored on servers in India, and locked with your PIN on the phone. Our privacy policy explains exactly what we store and why. Read our full Privacy Policy

Does it work without internet?

Saved records and medicine reminders open without internet. Reading a new report needs a connection.

Will Medverse tell me what illness I have?

No. Medverse explains what your reports and prescriptions say, in simple words. Only your doctor can diagnose and decide treatment.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer. An iPhone version is not available yet.
