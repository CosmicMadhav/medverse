EVERYTHING INSIDE

What’s in the app

  • Reports in simple words

    English or Hindi, with every value shown next to its normal range.

  • Read aloud

    For parents who would rather listen than read.

  • Two doctors, side by side

    Where they agree and where they differ, shown without taking sides.

  • Medicine clash warning

    Warns you when two doctors prescribe the same kind of painkiller.

  • No repeat tests

    Tells you if a recent report already has the result a doctor is asking for again.

  • Questions for your visit

    Share them on WhatsApp or print them, so nothing gets forgotten at the visit.

  • Emergency health card

    Blood group, allergies, conditions and an emergency contact on one screen.

  • Locked with your PIN

    Keep private records behind a PIN, even on a shared phone.

  • ASHA worker mode

    Keep simple records for many families from one phone.

IN AN EMERGENCY

Hold to call for help.

SOS needs a deliberate press, so it can’t go off by accident, but it is always one hold away.

  1. Press and hold SOS for 1.5 seconds. A quick tap only shows a hint.
  2. A 5-second countdown starts with a loud siren and a big Cancel button, so a slip of the thumb doesn’t call anyone.
  3. If you don’t cancel, your phone’s dialer opens with 108 ready, and the app shows the patient’s blood group, allergies, conditions and medicines so you can read them out.

Try it on the phone at the top: hold SOS.

OUR PROMISE

We explain. Doctors decide.

MedVerse helps you understand your records. It does not diagnose, it does not prescribe, and it never says which doctor is right.

Urgent values go to a doctor

If a result is dangerous, like potassium above 6.0, MedVerse shows “see a doctor today” and doesn’t try to explain it away.

Every explanation shows its source

Each plain sentence links back to the line it came from in your report.

No doctor is ranked

Differences are shown side by side so you can talk them through with your doctor.

Stored in India, visible only to you

Your records live in a private account on servers in Mumbai. Only you can open them, and sensitive records can be hidden behind a PIN.

QUESTIONS FAMILIES ASK

Before you download

Is MedVerse free?

Yes, it’s free to download and use.

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

You install it as an APK file straight from this page, because it isn’t on the Play Store yet. Android will ask your permission to install it, which is normal. Only download it from here.

Who can see my family’s records?

Your records are kept in a private account on servers in Mumbai, India. Only you can open them, and sensitive records can be hidden behind a PIN. To explain a report, its photo and text are read by AI services (Google Cloud Vision and Groq). Our privacy policy has the details. Read our full Privacy Policy

Does it work without internet?

No. MedVerse needs internet to load your records and to read new reports. The SOS button opens your phone’s dialer for 108, which works wherever you have a mobile network.

Will MedVerse tell me what illness I have?

No. It explains what your reports and prescriptions already say. Only your doctor can diagnose you or decide your treatment.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with Android 7.0 or newer. An iPhone version is not available yet.

How do I scan a report or prescription?

Take a clear photo of your report or prescription with the app. MedVerse reads the text (this needs internet) and explains the medical terms in plain words.

Can I manage records for my whole family?

Yes. Add separate profiles for your parents, children and spouse, and keep each person’s records, medicines and doctor visits in one app.

How does the two-doctor comparison work?

Add prescriptions from two doctors and MedVerse lists what they agree on, like the tests, and what is different, like the medicines. It also flags two similar painkillers.
