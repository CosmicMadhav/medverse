Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: Medverse stores your family’s health records solely to explain, organise, and compare them for you in plain language. Your records are encrypted, never sold to third parties or advertisers, and protected on your phone with your personal PIN. You can export or delete your data at any time.

1. Who We Are

Medverse is developed by Preeti Maheshwari and Madhav Laddha as part of an academic research initiative at APC Post Graduate College, Pratapgarh, Rajasthan, India (presented at IEEE WIE ILS 2026). Contact email: madhavladdha@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

  • Family Profiles: Names, age, relation, and basic profile info you enter to organise family health timelines.
  • Medical Documents: Photos and scans of prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries, and the OCR text extracted from them.
  • Health Timeline Data: Medicines, dose schedules, reminders, and doctor appointments you manage.
  • Emergency Card Details: Blood group, allergies, emergency contacts, and critical medical history for the SOS card.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information is used exclusively to power the app’s core functionality:

  • Translating and explaining lab values and prescription instructions into simple English and Hindi.
  • Organising chronological health journeys and medicine adherence reminders.
  • Comparing multiple doctor opinions side-by-side.
  • Enabling emergency health card access when you activate SOS.

4. Data Storage & Security

Medverse complies with the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023). All sensitive health records stored on your device are encrypted and locked behind your PIN. Offline-first architecture ensures your records remain accessible on your device without continuous internet access.

5. Sharing & Third Parties

We believe health information belongs solely to you and your family:

  • We never sell, rent, or monetize your health data.
  • We never share your data with insurance companies, employers, or ad networks.
  • Data is only shared when you explicitly choose to export or show a report to your doctor or caregiver.

6. Your Rights & Control

  • Access & Edit: View and update any profile or record directly within the app.
  • Deletion: Delete individual records, family members, or your entire app data at any time.
  • Grievances: For inquiries regarding your data, contact madhavladdha@gmail.com.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Medverse explains medical records to improve communication between families and healthcare professionals. It does not diagnose diseases, recommend prescriptions, or substitute for professional medical care. In a medical emergency, immediately call 112 or visit the nearest hospital.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published here with an updated revision date.
